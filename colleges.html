Largest US Universities

Accredited four-year colleges and universities in the United States, including universities that teach mostly online. Search by name, abbreviation, or city, filter by state or type, or click a column heading to sort.

Undergraduate enrollment is the fall 2023 headcount reported to the U.S. Department of Education (IPEDS), rounded to the nearest thousand. Campus-based universities are counted at their main campus. Online-first universities are included when they are accredited and award bachelor's degrees; they are marked Online, and their headquarters city is shown. Arizona State University's online programs are listed separately from its campuses. Colleges that mostly award two-year degrees are left out.
